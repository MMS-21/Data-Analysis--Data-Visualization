## 🛍 Deep Dive: Category Analysis

### 🏆 The Winner: Food & Beverages

- **Performance**: Market leader in revenue (`$56,145`) and profit (`$2,674`).
- **Insight**: Proves that scale and margin can coexist. Successful model of turn velocity and basket attachment.

### 📉 Silent Killers: Sports & Electronics

- **Performance**: High Revenue, **Low Margin**.
- **Impact**: These categories act as a drag on overall profitability.
- **Action Needed**: Immediate repricing or range rationalization.

### 🚀 Untapped Potential: Health & Beauty

- **Status**: Currently ranked **#Last**.
- **Opportunity**: High growth potential via cross-merchandising.
- **Strategy**: Bundle with high-frequency Food & Beverage purchases.

## 👥 Customer Insights

### Demographics & Behavior

- **Gender Split**: 👨 **Males (57.1%)** vs. 👩 Females (42.9%).
  - _Strategic Note_: Planograms and media should lean towards the male demographic driver.
- **Payment Methods**:
  - 💵 **Cash**: 34.4%
  - 📱 **Ewallet**: 34.5%
  - 💳 **Credit Card**: Low (High operational risk/cost).
  - _Action_: Incentivize digital/Ewallet payments.

### Satisfaction Gap

- **Current Rating**: 7/10 (Average).
- **Goal**: Convert "Passively Satisfied" (6-7s) into **Promoters (8-10s)**.
- **Plan**: Implement a closed-loop feedback system with 24-hour issue resolution.

## 🛠 Turnaround Blueprint (Q2 Goals)

**Mission: Rescue Margin**
Targeting a GPM uplift to **8.0%** in Q2.

### 1. 30-Day COGS Reset

- Renegotiate supplier terms & volume-tiered contracts.
- Implement dynamic pricing on key SKUs (especially Sports/Electronics).

### 2. Elevate Health & Beauty

- **Goal**: +25% Category Velocity.
- **Tactic**: Margin-neutral bundles (e.g., "Buy Food, get deal on Beauty").
- **Placement**: High-traffic end-caps & near checkouts.

### 3. Customer Loop

- **Trigger**: Auto-survey for scores of 6-7.
- **Response**: Real-time manager alerts for service recovery.
